Abandoned Outpost by schwarnhild
Source: https://schwarnhild.itch.io/abandoned-outpost-16x16-sci-fi-platformer-asset-pack
Original PNG files and license.txt are preserved without changes.

Unity settings: Sprite (2D and UI), Point filter, 16 Pixels Per Unit,
no mipmaps, no texture compression, Full Rect mesh.
Map tiles in tilesets/ are sliced on a 16 x 16 grid.
Other sprite sheets use the frame dimensions in their original filenames.
Transparent grid cells are omitted. r00 is the top row, c00 the left column.

To paint a map: open Window > 2D > Tile Palette, create a palette,
expand a tileset PNG, and drag its sliced sprites into the palette.
Characters and moving props include source frames, without animation controllers.
See license.txt for the author's terms.
